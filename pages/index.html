How it works

You don't need to know how to pose or plan any of this. Here's how I'll guide us through:

  • Understand what you actually need

    We start with what's coming up for you in real life: fundraising, hiring, new website, or speaking opportunities. We'll talk through how you want to come across, and I'll recommend the right session, timing, and shot list to cover you.

  • Show up to a shoot that's ready for you

    By the time you arrive, we'll have a clear plan for the images we need to make, and a quiet space set up for it. You won't have to figure out what to do on camera. I'll be guiding you the whole way through.

  • Leave with photos you'll actually use

    You'll review a curated selection, not a giant folder of lookalike shots. I'll help you choose images that make sense for places like LinkedIn, your pitch deck, publicity, and anywhere else you need to show up professionally.
